Executive Biography

A legacy of visionary management, regional media innovation, and self-sustaining community empowerment.

Hajji Karim Kaliisa brings over 22 years of executive experience, consistently delivering high-impact governance and strategic growth across NGO & Media Administration, Executive Management, Management Consulting, Media Entrepreneurship, and Information Communication Technology.

His career is characterized by an unwavering dedication to inclusive community development. Hajji Karim has spearheaded state-of-the-art media and telecommunications infrastructure, forged cross-continental technology partnerships, and mobilized civil society across Uganda and the broader East, Central, and Southern African regions.

A pioneering developer of regional self-reliance, he has championed initiatives that established academic centers, healthcare units, and civic infrastructure without reliance on external foreign aid, demonstrating the immense power of disciplined local leadership and collective purpose.
