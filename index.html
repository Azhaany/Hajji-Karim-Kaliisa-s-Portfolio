About

Biography

Hajji Karim Kaliisa brings more than 22 years of distinguished experience in business management, delivering excellent results across NGO and Media Administration, Executive Management, Management Consulting, Media Entrepreneurship, and Information Communication Technology.

His career is defined by a steadfast commitment to enhancing development for all, leveraging media and technology as powerful tools for community transformation across Uganda and the broader East, Central, and Southern African region.

A visionary leader who has pioneered media infrastructure projects, forged international partnerships, and mobilized communities toward self-confidence and sustainable development — establishing schools, health units, and social centers without reliance on foreign aid.
